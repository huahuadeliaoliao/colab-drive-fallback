Terms of use

Colab Drive Fallback is an independently maintained, personal-use desktop OAuth configuration, not a public hosted storage service. Effective date: 8 November 2026.

Authorized personal use

Use is limited to the account owner's own authorized file-access workflow. Do not use this configuration to access another person's account without permission, evade account security checks, or bypass Google or compute-provider policies. Google Drive, Google Colab, rclone and any selected hosting or compute service remain subject to their respective applicable terms.

Operation and limitations

The owner decides which files and trusted programs may use the mount. Read/write authorization can permit modification and deletion of Drive files. Keep independent copies of irreplaceable data and verify that uploads have completed before releasing a temporary runtime. This personal configuration does not provide a service-level guarantee of availability, throughput, credential lifetime, or training recovery.

Privacy and discontinuing use

The privacy policy explains Google data access and storage. The owner may stop using the configuration at any time by stopping its mounts, revoking the Google Account connection, and deleting local credentials or cache as appropriate. Those actions do not automatically delete files already stored in Drive.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the project's issue tracker. Never publish credentials, private account identifiers or private files in an issue.
